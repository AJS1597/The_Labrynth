Whatever lies behind the bronze slab to the north, this stair is the true way in. It descends past the foundations of the labyrinth into a long hall of iron braziers, each lit with a sickly green flame. At the far end stand huge double doors of oak and bronze, barred from your side. Beyond them, something vast shifts its weight and breathes. To the east, a low arch loops back up toward the gallery.
