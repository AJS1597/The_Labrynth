As you go walk down the long corridor you heard the low rumbling coming from, You enter a large chamber of sorts, its use being unknown to you. In the center, is a grate of which the low rumbling noise appears to be emanating. The walls are intricately carved, with inscriptions of a bipedal bull, And men holding swords. It seems to depict the men fighting and being slain by the beast. There are stairs descending in every which way.North East West South
