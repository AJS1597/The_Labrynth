YOU HAVE SLAIN THE MINOTAUR

The bar groans as you lift it away. The doors swing wide into a round chamber, and there he is — the Minotaur, black in the green brazier-light, taller than any man, his horns wide as a yoke. He charges, and you set your feet. What follows is a blur of bronze and blood and bellows, but when it is over, you are the one left breathing.

You are the first to walk out of the labyrinth alive. Somewhere far above, dawn is coming.
